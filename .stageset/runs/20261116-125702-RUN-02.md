## Result

PASS

## Facts

- The stage sum explains TTFA. The gap between compute and TTFA p95 is
  0.001 ms at B=128 and B=160.
- The IPC and admission cost is 0.56 to 0.72 ms. It is not the gap.
- Text prep is the largest stage: 65.4 ms p50 at B=128 and 84.7 ms p50 at B=160.
- First-emit iteration costs 25.9 ms p50 at B=128 and 34.0 ms p50 at B=160.
  This cost grows with B and adds to every row after the first.
- GPU util is 71% mean. The GPU is not saturated. This supports a CPU/launch limit.
- B=160 p95 TTFA is 216.1 ms with 800/800 rows under 350 ms in this clean run.
- `pip install -e /kaggle/working/pocket-tts --no-deps` makes `pocket_tts`
  importable in this session.

## Guesses

- The earlier 225 to 350 ms p95 at B=128 came from the in-process load
  generator and the `torch.cuda.synchronize()` call in the `bench_batch_server`
  `on_chunk` callback. This run does not use either. The guess is unproven
  until a paired run confirms it.
- The 50 to 180 ms "non-model" gap is measurement bias, not scheduler waste.

## Assumptions checked

- A-30 - `time.perf_counter()` is system-wide on this host.
  Result: CONFIRMED. Parent `t_send` and child stage times give a queue cost of
  0.56 to 0.72 ms. A clock mismatch would give a large or negative value.
- A-31 - A separate process removes the load-generator CPU bias.
  Result: CONFIRMED. CPU util mean is 32.8%. The parent does no GPU work.
- A-32 - The stage marks add less than 1 ms to TTFA.
  Result: CONFIRMED. The stage sum matches TTFA within 0.001 ms p95.
