## Result

FAIL for CHECK-LEVERS-INPROC-02. The interleaved in-process probe is biased by sustained thermal/load drift and does not rank configs reliably.

## Facts

- Under sustained load on T4 FP16, B=128 with voice 3.0/cap 16 does not meet 350 ms: p95 ~392 ms after warmup, ~103/128 rows under 350 ms.
- Under sustained alternating load, B=96 with voice 4.0/cap 16 meets 350 ms in every burst (p95 276-341 ms, 96/96 under 350 ms). Voice 3.0/cap 16 meets p95 < 350 ms (316-336 ms) but drops 0-2 rows in some bursts (94-96/96).
- The 24-config interleaved probe produced much higher p95 than short focused runs and reversed the 2.0 vs 4.0 order at B=96 cap 16. Long interleaved runs are not a valid ranking method on this GPU.

## Guesses

- Thermal or power throttling and allocator pressure during 72 back-to-back runs raised latency across all configs.

## Assumptions checked

- A-21: one process removes cross-process bias. PARTIAL. It removes process-start bias but adds sustained-load drift when runs are long.
- A-22: interleaved rounds remove order bias. PARTIAL. Interleaving does not remove thermal drift.
